    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    What currency are prices in?

    All prices are in US dollars (USD), and payment is in USD. Prices are the same on the Korean and English pages.

    How do I pay?

    Online payment isn’t connected yet. After you apply, we’ll send available times and how to pay. Applying doesn’t confirm a booking or complete a payment.

    The 60-minute focused UX review is paid in advance through a Stripe payment link. After payment, we’ll email you about scheduling and how to share your materials.

    For the UX diagnostic sprint, we’ll explain how to pay once the scope is confirmed.For the UX diagnostic sprint, you pay the quoted amount through a Stripe payment link once the scope and quote are confirmed. For the complex product flow improvement sprint, we’ll explain how to pay once the scope is confirmed.For the complex product flow improvement sprint, you pay the quoted amount through a Stripe payment link once the scope and quote are confirmed.

    Can I pay from South Korea?

    Yes. You can pay for the 60-minute UX Review through Stripe with a Visa or Mastercard enabled for international online payments. If checkout shows USD, your card issuer’s exchange rate and any foreign transaction fees may apply. If you’d prefer to pay in KRW or have trouble with your card, please contact us before paying. For sprint projects, we’ll send payment instructions after we agree on the scope and final price.

    What language are sessions in?

    Calls and reviews are held in Korean or English. You can choose either, whatever language the booking page is shown in.

    Will the review be recorded?

    Session recording isn’t included by default.

    Can we sign an NDA?

    If you need one, please ask before sharing any materials.

    Do you guarantee results?

    We don’t guarantee specific outcomes such as conversion gains. You get grounded judgment and clear priorities.

    What are the cancellation, rescheduling, and refund terms?

    They’ll be finalized and posted on Booking, cancellation, and materials before paid booking opens. Until then, we’ll explain them individually after you apply.
