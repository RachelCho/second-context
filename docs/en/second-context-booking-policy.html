    01

    How to book

    Free 30-minute call
    Book directly on Calendly. Book a 30-minute consultation (opens in a new tab)
    60-minute focused UX review
    Apply for a review, and we’ll send available times and how to pay. Your booking is confirmed after payment.
    UX diagnostic sprint and projects
    We start after agreeing on scope and a quote.
    Language
    Calls and reviews are held in Korean or English. You can choose either, whatever language the booking page is shown in.

    02

    Payment

    Currency
    Prices are in US dollars (USD), and payment is made in USD.
    Current status
    Online payment isn’t connected yet. After you apply, we’ll send available times and how to pay. Applying doesn’t confirm a booking or complete a payment.The 60-minute focused UX review is paid through a Stripe payment link. After payment, we’ll email you about scheduling and how to share your materials. For the UX diagnostic sprint, you pay the quoted amount through a Stripe payment link once the scope and quote are confirmed. For the complex product flow improvement sprint, you pay the quoted amount through a Stripe payment link once the scope and quote are confirmed.

    04

    Sharing and handling materials

    What we’ll ask for
    • Product URL
    • The screens or prototype to review
    • Target users
    • The flow to review and your current questions
    • What you want to decide after the session
    Please don’t send
    Real personal data, payment details, or passwords. Anonymized screens, sample data, or a safe demo are enough.
    NDA
    If you need one, please ask before sharing any materials.
    Recording
    Session recording isn’t included by default. Recording terms will be posted once finalized. Pending operator confirmation
    Keeping and deleting materials
    How long materials are kept and how they’re deleted will be posted once finalized. Pending operator confirmation
    Use in marketing or portfolio
    No permission to use your materials or deliverables in marketing or a portfolio is granted automatically. Terms will be posted once finalized. Pending operator confirmation
